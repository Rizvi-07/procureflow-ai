# Fictional reimbursement rules

Every amount and deadline below is FICTIONAL demo content, not financial or legal advice.

## Receipts and deadline

Under this FICTIONAL policy, employees must attach an itemized receipt and submit an expense claim within 30 calendar days of the expense. Claims must state the business purpose. The current procurement demo does not implement expense claims or receipt uploads.

## Meal allowance

The FICTIONAL meal reimbursement limit is EUR 25 per person per business day. Alcohol is not reimbursable under this invented policy. Exceptional expenses require finance review; the assistant cannot authorize an exception or process a reimbursement.
