# Fictional approval policy

All values and identities in this document are FICTIONAL demo content, not real company policy.

## Manager threshold

For this FICTIONAL demo, purchase requests totaling up to EUR 1,500 require one manager review. Requests above EUR 1,500 require finance review in addition to manager review. These thresholds describe policy only; the demo application's manual workflow does not implement automatic threshold routing.

## Separation of duties

In this FICTIONAL policy, employees must not review their own requests. A chatbot cannot authorize, create, approve, or reject a purchase. The current application's manager identity is demo-only and does not enforce this policy through authentication.
