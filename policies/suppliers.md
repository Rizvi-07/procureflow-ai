# Fictional preferred suppliers

All supplier names, limits, and processes below are FICTIONAL demo content.

## Preferred supplier list

The FICTIONAL preferred suppliers are Northstar Demo Office for office equipment and Example Harbor Computing for laptops. These invented names do not identify actual approved vendors.

## Supplier exceptions

Under this FICTIONAL policy, an employee requesting a nonpreferred supplier must include a business justification and obtain manager review. A preferred supplier does not remove the purchase approval requirement. The current demo does not store suppliers or enforce supplier selection.
